## Slide 2: The Core Market Problem in B2B Trade
1. **Pervasive Payment Risk**: Buyers fear supplier non-delivery; vendors fear payment default or prolonged debtor days (avg 90-120 days in India).
2. **Fragmented Discovery**: Wholesalers lack unified, verified product catalogs with transparent bulk tiered pricing.
3. **Heavy Regulatory Overhead**: GST verification, TDS under Section 194-O, e-way bills, and multi-tier KYC cause high transactional friction.
4. **Lack of Digitized Trust**: Traditional B2B procurement relies on manual cheques, unbacked credit, and lengthy legal disputes.

---

## Slide 3: The Noryvanta Solution
- **Zero-Trust B2B Escrow Engine**: Buyer deposits funds into a neutral virtual escrow account; supplier manufactures/ships with guaranteed solvency; funds release instantly upon milestone verification.
- **Instant AI Listing Assistant**: Suppliers digitize complex technical catalogs in minutes using automated HSN classification and SEO attribute generation.
- **Sovereign Tiered Verification**: Multi-layered GSTIN, PAN, and corporate verification yielding instant credibility and higher transaction limits.
- **Unified Multi-Currency Gateway**: Seamless routing across domestic UPI, NEFT/RTGS, and international wire clearing.

## Slide 7: Business Model & Monetization
1. **Take Rate / Platform Commission**: 2.5% to 5.0% on gross transaction value (GMV) upon successful escrow release.
2. **Sovereign Supplier Subscriptions**: Premium vendor tiers ($49 - $299/mo) with boosted search rankings, lower commission rates, and priority escrow settlement.
3. **Value-Added Financial Services**: Invoice factoring, vendor working capital lines, and integrated freight insurance premiums.
4. **AI Listing & Catalog Services**: Enterprise API access for high-volume automated inventory synchronization.

## Slide 8: Technical Architecture & Reliability
- **Frontend**: Next.js 14/15, Tailwind CSS, i18n multilingual layer (English, Hindi, regional).
- **Backend Core**: Python 3.11+ FastAPI (Async, high-concurrency microservices).
- **Database & Cache**: PostgreSQL (Supabase / AWS RDS) with ACID transactions + Redis for sub-millisecond caching & rate-limiting.
- **Asynchronous Engine**: Celery workers for background tax e-invoicing, OTP dispatch, and milestone settlement.
- **Enterprise Security**: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), end-to-end TLS, AES-256 data encryption at rest, and immutable audit ledgers.
